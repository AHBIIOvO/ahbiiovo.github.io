# AHBIIOvO · Portfolio

A personal website showcasing my game development and interactive projects: **AI-Driven VR Museum**, **First-Person Shooter**, **OutRun**, and **BNBU 3D Campus**.

[Visit the website](https://ahbiiovo.github.io/)

The site supports English and Chinese, desktop and mobile layouts, image previews, and video demos.

## Meet the cat

- **Desktop:** move the mouse to wake the cat. It follows the pointer, runs and jumps across the title and project-card tops, and reaches toward the cursor.
- **Click or tap:** the cat rolls onto its back, closes its eyes, and gently flutters its paws. Small “meow~” text and hearts may float upward.
- **Mobile:** the cat walks and jumps only on the title, with idle and grooming animations. Tap to interact.
